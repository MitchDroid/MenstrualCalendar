Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Our Promise

🔒 Your Data Never Leaves Your Device

BloomCycle is a 100% offline, privacy-first menstrual health tracker. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal or health data. Period.

📱 Local-Only Storage

All data you enter — daily logs, cycle predictions, symptoms, moods, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using Android's Room database and DataStore, both within the app's private sandboxed directory.

  • No cloud sync
  • No external servers
  • No third-party storage

🚫 No Account Required

BloomCycle works without any account registration, email address, or personal identity information. You never need to sign in, create a profile, or share identifying information to use any feature.

📊 No Analytics or Tracking

We do not collect any form of:

  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
  • Third-party SDKs that monitor activity

🌐 No Internet Access

BloomCycle does not request internet permission. The app functions entirely offline. No data is ever transmitted over any network. You can verify this by checking the app's permissions in your device settings.

🔐 Security Features

BloomCycle offers optional security features to protect your data:

  • Biometric Lock — Require fingerprint or face authentication to open the app. Biometric data is handled entirely by your device's secure hardware; BloomCycle never accesses it.
  • Screen Security — Block screenshots and screen recordings of the app using Android's FLAG_SECURE.

📤 Your Data, Your Control

  • Export — You can export all your data at any time in CSV or text format.
  • Delete — One-tap permanent deletion of all data from Settings. When deleted, data is immediately and irreversibly removed.
  • Uninstall — Uninstalling BloomCycle permanently deletes all data from your device. No residual data, no external backups.

📋 Permissions

BloomCycle requests only two optional permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — For period reminders, daily log reminders, and fertile window alerts. Optional; the app works without it.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — For precise notification timing. Optional; gracefully falls back if not granted.

No camera, storage, location, contacts, microphone, or internet permissions are requested.

👶 Children's Privacy

BloomCycle is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children, as the app does not collect data from anyone.

📝 Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Since BloomCycle collects no data and has no server component, material changes are unlikely.

💬 Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy or the app's data handling, the entire codebase is available for audit. Your trust matters.
